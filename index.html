About Us

Who We Are

PPGen is a national network of youth reproductive justice advocates working within Planned Parenthood's advocacy branch. Rather than providing healthcare services ourselves, we engage in the advocacy that makes reproductive healthcare possible.

🏥

Reproductive Justice

We believe in the fundamental right to have a child if you want, or not if you don't — on your own terms. This includes safe pregnancy, birth, parenting, and adoption experiences.

✊

Intersectionality

We acknowledge the deep-rooted intersections between reproduction and marginalized identity, and are especially responsive to the needs of the most marginalized communities.

🔗

Coalition Building

We serve as a coalition partner to campus, local, state, national, and international reproductive justice efforts — connecting students with the broader movement.

Our Work

What We Do

PPGen mobilizes advocates, educates the campus community on sexual and reproductive health, distributes sexual health materials (e.g., Plan B) in partnership with WGRC/FORCE, and builds partnerships across Tucson and beyond.

71+ Plan B doses distributed
10+ Supporter cards collected
4+ Campus events & tablings

Our advocacy covers: sex education, STI testing, abortion access, domestic violence prevention, maternal health, an end to forced sterilization, privacy rights, sexual liberation, and the rights of marginalized communities.
