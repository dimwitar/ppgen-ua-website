Planned Parenthood Generation Action

University of Arizona student advocates for reproductive justice, education, and action

Who We Are

PPGen is a national network of youth reproductive justice advocates working within Planned Parenthood’s advocacy branch.

What We Do

  • Mobilize students for reproductive justice advocacy
  • Educate the campus community on sexual and reproductive health
  • Share resources and build partnerships across campus and Tucson
